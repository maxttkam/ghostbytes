# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

GhostBytes contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation
welcoming, respectful, and constructive for everyone, regardless of experience,
identity, background, or technical viewpoint.

Good-faith disagreement about implementation, design, or project direction is
welcome. Discussion should focus on the work rather than the person, and should
leave room for questions, corrections, and different perspectives.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
  project spaces.
- Give feedback that is specific, relevant, and focused on improving the work.
- Assume good intent while addressing mistakes directly and clearly.
- Protect other people's privacy and do not publish private conversations,
  credentials, personal information, or sensitive files.
- Follow the project's contribution, security, and licensing requirements.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language or attention that is unwelcome;
- deliberate disruption, trolling, or repeated bad-faith arguments;
- publishing private information without permission;
- impersonation, plagiarism, or misrepresentation of contributions; and
- encouraging or facilitating behavior that violates this policy.

Security vulnerabilities and sensitive data must not be posted publicly. Follow
the private reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or edit violating content, close discussions,
request changes, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a
participant. Actions will be proportionate to the behavior and may be taken
without a public debate about private circumstances.

Maintainers are expected to apply these standards consistently and to recuse
themselves from enforcement decisions involving a conflict of interest.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through a direct,
non-public channel. Include links or relevant context, dates, participants,
and any supporting evidence, while avoiding unnecessary personal or sensitive
information.

Please do not use public issues or pull requests for conduct reports. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as practical, and retaliation against anyone
who makes a good-faith report is not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests,
reviews, chats, events, and other spaces where someone is representing
GhostBytes or participating as part of the project community.

## Acknowledgment

This policy is based on common open-source community standards and is intended
to support a safe, respectful, and productive GhostBytes community.
